About Azimut

Azimut Yachts is an Italian builder of flybridge, sport and long range motor yachts, founded in 1969 by Paolo Vitelli and part of the Azimut Benetti Group. The current range runs from the 12.9 m Verve 42 to the 43.6 m Grande 44M across seven series.

Azimut builds in Italy, at Avigliana, Savona, Viareggio, Livorno and Fano, and in Itajaí, Brazil. Which yard built a given model depends on its size and series; the model page carries the country for each.
